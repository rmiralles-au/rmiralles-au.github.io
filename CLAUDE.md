# Portfolio site (rmiralles-au.github.io)

Rey's personal portfolio: a showcase of the finance and AI tools he built at his internship, plus
his own projects. Personal, not RJMC: nothing from RJMC's private files goes here, and nothing
from the NDA client folder ever does. Read `NEXT_STEPS.md` first.

- Live at https://rmiralles-au.github.io/ from the `main` branch of the public repo
  `rmiralles-au/rmiralles-au.github.io`. Every push to `main` publishes. Commit and push only
  when Rey says the change is ready; preview first with headless Chrome from this folder.
- Static site, no build step: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `logo.svg`,
  `thumbnail.svg`. Edit in place.
- House style follows Rey's brand rules in `_claude-setup/CLAUDE.md` (dark, white letters, no em
  dashes, no invented results). Figures about his work come from `_claude-setup/ME.md` only.
